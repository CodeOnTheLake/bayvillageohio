About

Meet Jen Halaska

A Bay Village resident helping buyers and sellers across Greater Cleveland.

Bay Village · Greater Cleveland

Hello, I'm Jen.

Bay Village isn't just a market I work in — it's where I live. Living here gives me an everyday, on-the-ground feel for its neighborhoods, lakefront parks and schools, and I bring that local perspective to every client I work with.

My specialty is people who are new to the area. If you're moving to Cleveland for a job, family or a fresh start, I'll help you compare the west side Cleveland suburbs and the wider metro, from commute and schools to lake access, budget and lifestyle, then find the right home. See my relocation guide.

While Bay Village is home base, I help buyers and sellers throughout Greater Cleveland, including Westlake, Rocky River, Lakewood, Avon Lake, Fairview Park, North Olmsted and Avon.

I'm a member of the Erin Knapp team in Westlake, and I'm licensed with Howard Hanna Real Estate Services.
