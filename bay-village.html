The lakefront

Bay Village sits along the southern shore of Lake Erie in western Cuyahoga County, bordering Westlake, Rocky River and Avon Lake. Lake Road (US Route 6) follows the shoreline through the city, connecting its two signature lakefront parks.

Huntington Reservation

Part of Cleveland Metroparks since 1925, Huntington Reservation includes Huntington Beach — a public swimming beach reached by staircases down the bluff — along with picnic areas overlooking the lake, wooded trails along Porter Creek, a historic water tower and Karen's Way Play Space. The reservation is also home to Lake Erie Nature & Science Center (with its Schuele Planetarium) and BAYarts, the community arts campus.

Cahoon Memorial Park

Next door, Cahoon Memorial Park stretches from Lake Erie to Wolf Road. It has a paved path along the lakefront bluff, sports fields, a skate park, a disc golf course and the historic Rose Hill Museum, operated by the Bay Village Historical Society.

Schools

Bay Village is served by the Bay Village City School District, whose campuses include Normandy Elementary (K–2), Westerly Elementary (3–4), Bay Middle School (5–8) and Bay High School (9–12), plus the Glenview Center for preschool. Confirm current boundaries and programs directly with the district at bayvillageschools.com.

Homes in Bay Village

Most homes in Bay Village are owner-occupied single-family houses on established, tree-lined streets, ranging from mid-century colonials and ranches to lakefront and near-lake properties. Every street has its own character — which is where local knowledge makes a difference.
