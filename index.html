FAQ

Moving to Cleveland: common questions

Can Jen help if I'm moving to Cleveland from out of state?

Yes. Helping out-of-town buyers and families relocating to Cleveland is Jen's specialty. She starts by learning your commute, school, budget and lifestyle priorities, recommends the suburbs that fit, and then helps you find and buy the right home, including by video tour when you can't be here in person.

What are the best Cleveland suburbs for families?

It depends on your priorities, but many west side Cleveland suburbs pair strong schools with parks and lake access. On the 2024–25 Ohio School Report Card, Bay Village, Rocky River and Avon districts earned five stars, and Westlake, Avon Lake and Fairview Park earned 4.5. See the relocation guide for a side-by-side comparison.

Why do people choose Bay Village?

Bay Village combines Lake Erie shoreline, tree-lined streets (it's been a Tree City USA for 26 consecutive years), Huntington Beach, a five-star school district and a roughly 20–25 minute drive to Cleveland Hopkins Airport. Read 13 reasons to live in Bay Village.

Is Jen a Bay Village realtor?

Yes. Jen lives in Bay Village and is a licensed Ohio REALTOR® (License #2025007585) serving Bay Village and all of Greater Cleveland, including Westlake, Rocky River, Lakewood, Avon Lake, Fairview Park, North Olmsted and Avon.
