Meet Jennifer

A neighbor who knows the lake, the streets and the market.

Bay Village isn't just a market Jennifer works in — it's home. That everyday, on-the-ground familiarity with its neighborhoods, lakefront parks and schools shapes the advice she gives every client, from first-time buyers to longtime homeowners ready for what's next.

While Bay Village is home base, Jennifer serves all of Greater Cleveland, including Westlake, Rocky River, Lakewood, Avon Lake, Fairview Park, North Olmsted and Avon.
