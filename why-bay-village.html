Why Bay Village

Why live in Bay Village, Ohio?

Lake Erie on one side, tree-lined streets on the other, and a small-town spirit just minutes from Cleveland. Here's why Jen, and so many families, call Bay Village home.

At a glance

A lakefront hometown with big-city access

Bay Village pairs the feel of a close-knit village with parks, beaches and arts on the shore of Lake Erie, plus quick drives to the airport and downtown.

01

Tree-covered streets

Bay Village has been recognized as a Tree City USA by the Arbor Day Foundation for 26 consecutive years, and earned Tree City USA Growth Awards in 2023 and 2024. Mature canopies shade its established residential streets, and the city maintains an inventory of more than 3,000 city trees.

Source: City of Bay Village
02

Lake Erie views

The city's entire northern edge is Lake Erie shoreline. Lake Road (US Route 6) follows the water, and a paved path along the bluff at Cahoon Memorial Park offers some of the best sunset views on the west side.

Source: ODNR Lake Erie public access guide
03

Lake Erie Nature & Science Center

A local favorite for families: free general admission to live-animal exhibits and natural-history displays, a wildlife rehabilitation program for injured and orphaned animals, and the Schuele Planetarium.

Source: lensc.org
04

Huntington Beach & Reservation

Part of Cleveland Metroparks since 1925, Huntington Reservation includes Huntington Beach, a public swimming beach on Lake Erie, plus wooded trails along Porter Creek, lakeview picnic areas, the historic water tower and Karen's Way Play Space.

Source: Cleveland Metroparks
05

Kids ride bikes to school

Bay Village's compact, close-knit neighborhood schools make walking and biking a real option. The city runs a Safe Routes to School program, which upgraded five school-route intersections in 2023, and the schools hold an annual Bike to School Challenge.

Source: City of Bay Village; Bay Village City Schools
06

A short drive to the airport

Cleveland Hopkins International Airport (CLE) is roughly 9–10 miles away, typically about a 20–25 minute drive depending on traffic and your starting point.

Source: Route planners (OSRM, Travelmath)
07

Five-star public schools

Bay Village City Schools earned a five-star overall rating on the 2024–25 Ohio School Report Card. The district reports that its performance index ranks 4th in Cuyahoga County.

Source: Ohio Dept. of Education & Workforce; Bay Village City Schools
08

BAYarts & the former Huntington Playhouse

The BAYarts campus inside Huntington Reservation offers classes, galleries and events for artists of all ages. Its historic buildings include the former Huntington Playhouse, a community theater from 1958 to 2015 that BAYarts is renovating for cultural programming.

Source: BAYarts
09

Cahoon Memorial Park & Rose Hill Museum

The 116-acre park stretches from the lake to Wolf Road, with sports fields, a skate park, disc golf and the bluff-top path. It's also home to the Rose Hill Museum, run by the Bay Village Historical Society.

Source: ODNR; Bay Village Historical Society
10

Bay Days on the Fourth of July

Since the 1960s, the Kiwanis Club of Bay Village has hosted Bay Days at Cahoon Memorial Park: a multi-day Independence Day festival with rides, free gazebo concerts, a children's bike, trike and wagon parade, and fireworks on the Fourth.

Source: Bay Village Kiwanis
11

A safe community

Bay Village was named among Ohio's safest cities in SafeWise's 2025 ranking, based on FBI crime data.

Source: SafeWise 2025
12

Close to downtown Cleveland

Downtown Cleveland is about 15 miles east. Residents can be at a game, a show or the office in roughly 25 minutes, then back home by the lake.

Source: Route estimates
13

A brand-new library

The Cuyahoga County Public Library Bay Village Branch opened in 2022 at 27400 Wolf Road: two stories and 17,000 square feet with teen and children's rooms, a maker space with 3D printers, and a rooftop patio.

Source: City of Bay Village
Living here

Seasons, sunsets and Saturday mornings

Summer days at Huntington Beach, fall color on tree-lined streets, winter walks along the lake and the Fourth of July at Bay Days. Life in Bay Village follows the seasons.
